Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

Renu Design ("we", "our", "us") operates the Heapr mobile application and the renudesign.com website. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

When you use Heapr, we collect:

  • Account information — your email address and password when you sign up
  • Content you create — items, locations, categories, and photos you add to your inventory
  • Usage data — crash reports and basic analytics to improve the app (via Sentry)

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, or any data unrelated to the app's core functionality.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Heapr service
  • Authenticate your account and sync your data across devices
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app stability
  • Communicate with you about your account or service updates

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, with row-level security policies ensuring that only you can access your own data. Photos are stored in secure cloud storage. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • With service providers that help us operate the app (hosting, error tracking)
  • When required by law or to protect our legal rights

Your Rights

You can:

  • Access and export your data at any time through the app
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Update or correct your information within the app

Children's Privacy

Heapr is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email or through the app. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

About Renu Design

Who we are

Renu Design is an independent software studio. We design and develop mobile and web applications with a focus on clarity, reliability, and attention to detail.

Our approach

We believe software should be straightforward and respectful of your time. We prioritize data privacy, clean interfaces, and features that solve real problems — no bloat, no dark patterns.

Technology

We pick the right tool for each product: native Swift on iOS, cross-platform mobile where it fits, lightweight web apps on the edge, and Go for the terminal.

Based in

Toronto, Canada. We operate remotely and serve users worldwide.
